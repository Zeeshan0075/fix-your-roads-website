Questions

How do I open the next step?
Earn at least one star on a step to open the next one. Two stars on a step open Endless Rivalry there, on every step except Hamdan Street.
What do the road colours mean?
Teal lanes flow, amber lanes are slow and red lanes are jammed. Turn on Colour blind roads in Settings to add dashes to jammed lanes.
How do I move the map?
Drag with one finger to pan. Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn, and move two fingers up or down together to tilt.
Why was my edit refused?
Edits cost coins, and some do not fit: a building in the way, not enough room for a roundabout, or a change that would leave drivers with no way out. The message on screen says which.
Does the game need the internet?
No. Everything, including the daily challenge, works offline.
How do I start again?
Settings, then Reset progress. Stars, best scores and daily results are cleared; your settings stay.
Why does the game ask about Photos?
Only when you save a clip, so it can add the video to your library. It never reads your photos.
Is a real place wrong?
The places come from OpenStreetMap and are simplified for play. Tell us what looks wrong and where.
