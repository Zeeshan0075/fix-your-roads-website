Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Fix Your Roads does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not connect to any server. It works fully offline.

Your progress

Stars, best scores, daily challenge results and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Clips and your photo library

If you choose to save or share a clip of a run, the game adds that video to Photos or hands it to the share sheet you pick. It asks for permission only to add the clip and never reads anything from your photo library.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game contains no third party advertising, analytics or tracking code. Downloads and any purchases go through Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Map data

The road layouts come from OpenStreetMap and ship inside the app. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
